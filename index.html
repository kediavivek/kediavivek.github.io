Why I do this

One example

Onboarding Tracker

● SHIPPED · IN USE
What was going wrong

Associate onboarding was tracked manually across spreadsheets and chats — no single source of truth on status, blockers stayed invisible until they'd already delayed a milestone, and there was no audit trail on who changed what or when.

What I built
  • Role-based console with per-user permissions
  • Multi-associate checklists by phase, with blocker capture & timestamped resolution
  • Staffing/lifecycle board (Planned → Hired → At Risk)
  • Live dashboard, full audit log, CSV/Excel import-export, real-time sync
How I approached it

Built as a single-file application, self-directed — no dev team, no sprint cycle. I described the workflow, business rules and edge cases directly to Claude and Emergent, and iterated the logic and UI myself in days.

Why I think it matters

The useful part wasn’t the technology. It was turning a messy process into something the team could actually see and use.
