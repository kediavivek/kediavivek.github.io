Something I shipped

The onboarding tracker

Associate onboarding was being tracked across spreadsheets and chat threads. Nobody could say with confidence where a person was, blockers only surfaced after they'd cost a milestone, and there was no record of who changed what.

So I built a console for it: role-based access, checklists per phase, blockers captured with timestamps and a name attached, a staffing board from planned through at-risk, a live dashboard, an audit log, CSV in and out, real-time sync.

No dev team and no sprint cycle — I described the workflow, rules and edge cases to Claude and Emergent, then iterated the logic and interface myself. It went from request to go-live in four days against a 13.5-day scope.

Which is the actual point: if that's what one person can ship for an enterprise team, the small-business version is not a quarter of work.
